### 6. Acting & staging

- **The man** (navy suit, red tie, teal lapel, cream shirt, stubble, tall box head with a flat black hair cap): three states only — walking (arms swing, leash hand forward), arm yanked/lowered, and the leash-grip mime. Face is a drawn texture: asymmetric dot eyes with heavy lids, a short straight mouth. No mouth movement, one blink. He never looks at the tornado after f56 and never appears after f195.
- **The dog**: orange box body, huge square white eyes with black pupils, brown muzzle, red collar; trots on a taut red leash that sags realistically (secondary motion); in the funnel its legs hang, leash whips. It is the only character that emotes.
- **The tornado (offender)**: stacked low-poly grey rings forming a cone, lighter band segments rotating, jagged crown, small rock/dust skirt at the base, a cloud of confetti debris (cream, red, dark) orbiting at all times — indoors too. **No face, no eyes, no arms.** It "acts" only through props: white flag sprouting from the top, oversized white handcuffs clamped round the upper funnel (they read like spectacles and stay on in the turbine and grave shots), and spin speed. - **The jet** is the "police" here: no pilot visible, no people in any military shot.
- **Judge**: same head construction as the man (stubble, same eyes) + white curled wig, round wire glasses, black robe, white bands. One action: the gavel. Eyes stay on the dock.

### 10. Deviations from the known rules

**Formula**
- CONFIRMS: "This man <ordinary thing>" → nature does what it does → "That was <CHARGE>." → "So he called …" → literal over-reaction → institution → sentence → short coda.
- BREAKS: the call goes to "the U.S. military", not the police. There are **two** institution gags in a row (court, then supervised labour) instead of one. The coda is 6 words ("Oh, and the dog is dead."), not 2–5, and it is a callback to the victim rather than a comment on the offender.
- NEW: the over-reaction takes 9.6 s (f196–482, 8 shots = 33 % of the runtime); a three-step literal build "It hit the tornado. / It also hit the neighborhood. / All of it."; the offender *surrenders* (it is never caught); the lead disappears after 6.5 s.

**Lead never speaks; narrator voices everyone; offender is weather**
- CONFIRMS all three. No quoted captions at all — nobody "says" anything, even via the narrator.

**Median ≈ 1.0–1.2 s; action runs 0.4–0.6 s; last frame frozen 10–24 frames; no outro**
- Median: 1.38 s by eye (mean 1.31 s) — BREAKS the rule only if in-shot jolts are not counted as cuts; by the machine list it is 0.77 s (see §3). By eye there is only one sub-0.5 s shot; the fast run is 0.50 / 0.60 / 0.83 s.
- BREAKS freeze: the last shot is not frozen; it keeps pushing in with moving grass and leaf to the final frame (12 frames after the last word).
- CONFIRMS no outro (the TikTok card is not the creator's).
- NEW: action density is carried inside long shots (3–4 explosions per shot, one every ~12 frames) instead of by cutting.

**~79 % push in (+11 %/s); ~27 % fast arrival; roll 2–10°; motion blur**
- BREAKS push-in share: 13 of 22 (59 %, not ~79 %), with a much wider rate spread (+4 to ≈ +50 %/s; median ≈ +12–14 %/s).
- CONFIRMS fast arrivals: 5 of 22 (23 %), settling in 5–10 frames (slightly slower than 4–6).
- Roll: mostly within 5–10°, but shot 6 is ≈ 35–40° — BREAKS the range for vehicle action.
- CONFIRMS motion blur on fast moves (whip-orbit, fly-over, gavel, whip lunge).

**Charge plate**
- CONFIRMS: dark navy, top-centre, drops in on the charge word, overshoot/swing, settles in 6–7 frames.
- NOT SHOWN: no repeat/re-drop, no replacement, no strike-through (single charge).
- NEW / differs: no visible rope or chain; 2-frame overexposed text flash on arrival; whole-frame jolt on landing; plate spans ≈ 90 % of frame width.

**Captions**
- CONFIRMS: small white, dark outline, ~80 % height, hard swap, ≤ 2 lines.
- BREAKS: words per card run 1–4, median 3 (rule says 2–5; the 1-word "Oh," card is the exception); coral is **not** one keyword — 5 cards have none, 4 colour a two-word phrase, 1 colours two separate words.
- NOT SHOWN: quotes, profanity bleep, em dash.
- NEW: cards are cut to the voice phrase, not the picture (3 cards cross cuts); every silence is caption-free.

**Phone = 3D prop sliding up from the bottom (~5 frames); fake UI inserts; pause-bait props; red annotations**
- CONFIRMS 3D prop and speed (4 frames + 2 settle). Differs: it swings in from the bottom-right corner on an arc to the ear, held in the character's own hand, back toward camera.
- BREAKS/NOT SHOWN: no full-frame fake UI insert; no red arrows/circles. The HUD is an overlay on a 3D shot, not an insert.
- Pause-bait: weak — only the handle on five props (the tower one needs a pause to read). No hidden jokes in signage found.

**Police strobe; muzzle stars + brass; dust-brawl cloud**
- NOT SHOWN: no strobe, no guns, no brawl cloud. Replaced by missile trails, 3-stage fireballs with a glow disc, and white impact lines for the whip.

**Not covered by the list**
- Cloud white-out as a hidden transition; same-camera before/after match cut; in-shot step-zooms for UI beats; offender that "acts" only through attached props; the punished offender reused as background set-dressing in the coda; channel handle embedded on 5 props; a silent picture-only beat (1.5 s) immediately after the hook sentence.

### 11. Steal list

1. **Before/after match cut for collateral damage:** build the wide once, cut on the first word of the topper line ("All of it.") to the *identical* camera with the destroyed variant; hold ≥ 45 frames with the caption gone for the last 25–30.
2. **Stack hits inside one shot instead of cutting:** in a 45–52-frame shot fire 3–4 impacts 11–12 frames apart, each 3-stage (2–4-frame white-yellow core with a soft glow disc ≈ 60 % of frame width → 6–8-frame orange fireball → persistent dark puff smoke), and let old smoke stay so damage accumulates. Walk successive strikes toward the camera.
3. **Sync every physical hit to a stressed word at ±1 frame** (plate on the charge word, phone on "called", first missile on "fired", flash on "hit", gavel on "labor"), and add a 1–2-frame whole-frame kick on that frame; no other camera shake.
4. **Offender without a face acts through props:** give a weather/object offender one attached prop per beat (white flag from the top, oversized white handcuffs, a whip-driven speed change) rather than eyes or arms; keep the prop on in later shots as continuity.
5. **Vehicle montage in < 2 s:** three shots, 15 / 18 / 25 frames — glowing detail ECU, fast track along the body with 35–40° roll and readable markings for 4 frames, head-on take-off that passes over the lens with 3 full-smear frames.
6. **UI lock-on as a colour ramp:** corner brackets + centre diamond, white → yellow (7 frames) → orange (1) → red; fill the diamond and pop a red label on the last word; add two blur-free step-zooms of a few percent on the way. Total ≈ 2 s.
7. **Hidden transition through a full-frame element:** fly the subject into cloud, hold 4 fully white frames, come out on the new camera. Use where a hard cut between two aerial views would disorient.
8. **Scale brackets for an institution gag:** wide with the human at ≈ 4 % of frame height (17 frames) → MS on the action with a 7-frame pan-settle and +20 %/s push (42 frames) → back to the wide for the consequence (14 frames).
9. **Windup timing for a strike:** 8 frames crouch → 4 frames sweep back → 4-frame hold at the top → 2 smear frames → contact with 6–8 white radial lines + dust puff for 3 frames → 15–20 frames recovery. Gavel variant: 6 up, 2 hold, 2 smear, hit, 30-frame hold.
10. **Coda staging:** cut to the coda image 9–10 frames before the line, keep a slow +12 %/s push with idle motion (grass, one falling leaf) to the last frame, and place the punished offender small in the background so the last image carries both endings.
11. **Fill the silence after the hook with the victim's POV:** 1.5 s, no caption, on the one character that emotes (the dog in the funnel) before the deadpan charge line.
12. **Captions follow the voice, not the cut:** swap on the first word of each 1–4-word phrase, let a card run across a cut if the phrase does, clear it within ~20 frames of the last word, and leave silences caption-free; colour the pay-off noun phrase (1–2 words) and leave connective cards all white.
