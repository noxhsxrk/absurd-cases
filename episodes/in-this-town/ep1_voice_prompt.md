# ในเมืองนี้ EP1 · หาว (ฉบับยาว ~2 นาที) — Voice prompt (Gemini TTS)

Voice: **Rasalgethi** (Nox เลือก 2026-11-16 — เสียงประจำซีรีส์ในเมืองนี้)

## Style
A calm, informative Thai narrator telling the story of his own strange hometown in the first person (he was the kid in it), completely matter-of-fact, like a documentary guide explaining local customs, as if every rule is normal. Deadpan but not flat: clear dynamics, punch the key words, a little dry wonder. Never laugh. Natural spoken Thai, clear diction. Pace: brisk storytelling, about 2 minutes in total. A short breath at each comma (about a quarter of a second) and a short pause between sentences (about half a second); a longer pause (about one second) before sentence 15 and before sentence 28 (new chapters).

Sections:
- 1–14 the town and its rules: plain explaining, like showing a visitor around. Sentence 10 lowers the voice on "น่าเบื่อ" like a forbidden word. 13–14 slightly mysterious.
- 15–18 the new leader: the town is excited; the kid reports it flatly.
- 19–27 the disaster: counting slides, slow dread building; 23 grave; 24–26 dry list of consequences; 27 flat disbelief.
- 28–34 grandma's secret: quiet, low, intimate, like telling a secret at night.
- 35–41 the kid's move: determined; 37 the quoted line is spoken loud and clear into a megaphone, a beat after "หัวหน้าครับ"; 38–40 big and amazed, then 41 tired and flat.
- 42–45 aftermath: calm and dry; 45 slow, a tiny pause before "ภาคสอง"; stop clean, no trailing sound.
