# EP20 · แก๊งห้อง 24 ตอน 1 — ไม่มีใครเอาด้วย — Voice prompt (Gemini TTS, voice: Fenrir)

## Style
Thai male narrator, about 35, warm deep voice, like a dramatic TV crime-documentary host who takes a ridiculous story 100% seriously. Lively and expressive, NOT flat: punch the key words, real dynamics. Never laugh: the comedy comes from how seriously he treats it. Natural spoken Thai, clear diction. Pace: steady, never rushed — the whole read is about 28 seconds. A short breath at each comma (about half a second) and a clear pause between sentences (about one second). Sentences 1 to 5 are a grim prison report. Sentences 6 to 10 are a roll call of refusals, each one short, flat and final, like reading out a list of rejections. Sentence 11 is outraged again. Sentence 12 is cut off mid-word. Sentences 13 and 14 are a quiet, dry ending.

Delivery per sentence:
1 calm and heavy, like opening a prison documentary, a small weight on "ยี่สิบสี่"
2 slow and oppressive on "กำแพงคุก", then plain on "ขังเขาไว้ทั้งวัน"
3 outraged, a beat after "นั่นคือ", then big on "กักขังหน่วงเหนี่ยว!"
4 quick and decisive
5 matter-of-fact on "ผู้คุมหน้าห้องรับสาย", a beat, then flat and deadpan on "แล้ววางหู"
6 determined, a little conspiratorial, low on "แหกคุก"
7 short and flat: "พัดลม", a breath, "ส่ายหน้า"
8 short and flat: "โลก", a breath, "หมุนหันหลังให้"
9 short and flat: "ไฟในตู้เย็น", a breath, "เย็นชาใส่", a small cold stress on "เย็นชา"
10 slow and a little wounded: "ดวงจันทร์", a breath, "ไม่เคยหันหน้ามาหาเขา"
11 outraged, a beat after "นั่นคือ", then big on "เลือกปฏิบัติ!"
12 quick and decisive like sentence 4, but stop dead in the middle of the word on "ตำ" — no closing sound, no breath after, just silence
13 quiet and plain: "มีแค่ยุง", a breath, "ที่ตอบตกลง"
14 slow and dry, a tiny pause after "เซ็นสัญญา", flat on "ด้วยเลือดเขา"; stop clean, no trailing sound
