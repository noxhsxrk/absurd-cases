Clips: r1 "wrong neighbourhood / microaggression" (28 s), r2 "identity theft — the mirror twin" (31 s), r3 "earthquake → arrested Earth" (23 s),
r4 "negligence — the cloud" (65 s special), r5 "home invasion — the dark" (28 s). 576×1024 @ 30 fps. Every-frame strips were made for:
r2 0.0–1.6 s (brushing teeth → mirror reveal), r5 11.3–12.9 s (officers turn their heads), r5 3.35–4.95 s (the dark comes in),
r4 2.95–4.55 s (runs, slips in the rain, slides), r1 13.15–14.75 s (baton beating → ambulance arrives).

## Why it looks natural (and EP8 v1 did not)
1. **Render**: engine-grade (Blender EEVEE / Unity look). Soft area-light shadows, ambient occlusion in every crease, bounce light
   (walls tint faces), bloom on lamps, volumetric god-rays with floating dust (r2 bathroom), depth of field on close-ups, motion blur on
   EVERY fast move (hand brushing, ambulance driving in, paramedics jumping out). Surfaces are painted: brush-stroke cloth, ink edge lines
   on box heads, stubble dots, paper grain. Nothing is a flat single colour.
2. **Motion**: real mocap/keyed acting with weight. The slip (r4) is a ballistic arc: run → foot goes → body rotates mid-air, legs fly
   → hard landing → slides toward camera with spray. Idle people are never still: head turns lead with the eyes, eased over 8–12 frames,
   hands gesture with blur. Falls/impacts read as physics, not tweens.
3. **Camera as an operator**: it tracks the action (side-tracking the runner, panning with the ambulance), re-frames, lands with ease;
   handheld-smooth drift on calm shots. Wide lens close to faces for comedy. Camera whips/blur bridge cuts.
4. **One action per shot, staged for the lens**: clean silhouettes, nothing between the lens and the subject, the light leads the eye.
5. **Abstract offenders are shown through the world's own physics**: "the dark" (r5) is never a black puppet — it is the light in the
   room going out, a black void in the doorway, a black square on the WANTED poster. → EP8's shadow must be a real cast shadow from the
   scene light (soft, lit like everything else) that moves on its own; only after it is "peeled" may it become a thin black film.

## Production rules taken from this
- Look-dev the offender alone first and get Nox's OK before building shots.
- Animatic (low-res, full motion) of the whole episode before polish.
- Per shot: check every frame at the key actions (not one still per shot); no occluders; offender always readable.
- Independent reviewer (fresh agent) compares against these clips before anything is sent.
