# EP23 · แก๊งห้อง 24 ตอน 4 — แผนรมผู้คุม — Voice prompt (Gemini TTS, voice: Fenrir)

## Style
Thai male narrator, about 35, warm deep voice, like a dramatic TV crime-documentary host who takes a ridiculous story 100% seriously. Lively and expressive, NOT flat: punch the key words, real dynamics. Never laugh: the comedy comes from how seriously he treats it. Natural spoken Thai, clear diction. Pace: steady, never rushed — the whole read is about 38 seconds. A short breath at each comma (about half a second) and a clear pause between sentences (about one second). Sentence 1 is calm and low, picking up where last episode left him. Sentence 2 is the reveal of a secret plan, like a heist film. Sentence 3 is the plan going off, proud and big. Sentences 4 and 5 are the turn: matter-of-fact, then sinking. Sentence 6 is the usual outrage. Sentence 7 is quick and decisive. Sentences 8 to 10 are a by-the-book police response, firm and official. Sentences 11 and 12 are the dry punchline. Sentences 13 and 14 are dry and bureaucratic. Sentence 15 is a hushed, suspenseful cliffhanger, like the last line before "to be continued".

Delivery per sentence:
1 calm and low, a tiny pause after "ชายคนนี้", then flat and a little pitiful on "ยังติดหน้าต่างครึ่งตัว"
2 a quiet reveal on "กลิ่นห้องยี่สิบ", a breath, then sly and secret on "คือแผนรมผู้คุม"
3 a breath after "เช้านั้น", then proud and big on "ทุเรียนปล่อยกลิ่นเต็มที่"
4 a short stop after "แต่พัดลมห้องสิบเก้า", then plain and matter-of-fact on "ส่ายหน้าตามปกติ"
5 sinking: "เป่ากลิ่นทั้งหมด", a breath, then flat on "เข้าห้องยี่สิบสี่"
6 outraged and formal, a beat after "นั่นคือ", then big and heavy on "ใช้อาวุธเคมี!"
7 quick and decisive
8 firm and official: "ผู้คุมใส่หน้ากากกันแก๊ส", a breath, then steady on "เดินฝ่าเข้ามา"
9 brisk: "จับพัดลม", a breath, then dry on "ใส่กุญแจมืออีกคู่"
10 deadly serious: "ห่อพลาสติก", a breath, then precise on "ติดป้ายวัตถุอันตราย"
11 proud on "แผนรมผู้คุม", a breath, then satisfied on "รมได้ทุกคน"
12 slow and dry, a beat before it: "ยกเว้นผู้คุม"
13 dry: "ผู้คุมส่องห้องยี่สิบสี่", a breath, then flat on "เห็นแต่ควัน"
14 bureaucratic: "เลยประทับตรา", a beat, then stamped down on "หลบหนีแล้ว"
15 quiet, slower, suspense creeping in: "คืนนั้น", a breath, "ผู้ต้องขังคนใหม่", a beat, then low and ominous on "ถูกพาเข้าห้องยี่สิบสี่"; stop clean, leave it hanging, no trailing sound
