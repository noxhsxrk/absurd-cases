# EP21 · แก๊งห้อง 24 ตอน 2 — แหกคุกได้ครึ่งตัว — Voice prompt (Gemini TTS, voice: Fenrir)

## Style
Thai male narrator, about 35, warm deep voice, like a dramatic TV crime-documentary host who takes a ridiculous story 100% seriously. Lively and expressive, NOT flat: punch the key words, real dynamics. Never laugh: the comedy comes from how seriously he treats it. Natural spoken Thai, clear diction. Pace: steady, never rushed — the whole read is about 38 seconds. A short breath at each comma (about half a second) and a clear pause between sentences (about one second). Sentences 1 and 2 are a secret escape plan, low and conspiratorial, like a heist film. Sentences 3 to 5 are the usual outrage, then a quick embarrassed take-back. Sentences 6 to 8 build like the escape is really working, more and more hopeful. Sentences 9 to 11 are outrage again, bigger than before. Sentence 12 is short and grim. Sentences 13 to 15 are an action-movie raid. Sentence 16 is a dry punchline. Sentences 17 and 18 turn into a hushed, suspenseful cliffhanger, like the last line before "to be continued".

Delivery per sentence:
1 calm and low, conspiratorial, a small weight on "แหกคุก"
2 secretive and serious, as if this is a brilliant plan: "ให้ยุงดูดเลือด", a breath, "จนผอมพอลอดซี่กรง"
3 outraged, a beat after "นั่นคือ", then big on "ทำร้ายร่างกาย!"
4 quick and decisive
5 quick, a little embarrassed, lower, like taking it back under his breath
6 growing, a weight on "ล้านตัว"
7 slow and amazed on "เขาค่อยๆ แฟบ", then wondering on "จนแบนเป็นกระดาษ"
8 hopeful and building, almost triumphant, then a small stop on "ครึ่งตัว"
9 flat, then a pained weight on "คัน"
10 outraged, a beat after "นั่นคือ", big on "ทำร้ายร่างกาย", a beat, then even bigger on "ล้านกระทง!"
11 quick and decisive, the same as sentence 4
12 short, grim and final: "คราวนี้", a breath, "ไม่ถอน"
13 big and serious, as if it is a military operation, punch "ทั้งแดน"
14 fast: "ยุงหนีหมด", a breath, then dry on "หอบเลือดเขาไปด้วย"
15 proud on "จับได้ตัวเดียว", a beat, then precise on "ใส่กุญแจมือจิ๋ว"
16 slow and dry, a tiny pause after "ชายคนนี้", flat on "แหกคุกได้ครึ่งตัว"
17 quiet, slower, suspense creeping in: "คืนนั้น", a breath, "ครึ่งตัวข้างนอก"
18 hushed and ominous, a beat after "มีอะไรลื่นๆ", then low and slow on "มาจับมือเขาไว้"; stop clean, leave it hanging, no trailing sound
