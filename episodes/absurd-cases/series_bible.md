## Open hooks
- EP6: นช. สะพานลอย is chained outside the prison gate for life (stands in the sun); the mosquito's million relatives arrived to visit (paid off EP1's hook) and must walk over the bridge, 200 baht fine per mosquito; cell 18 is empty again (couldn't fit the bridge).
- EP5: two escapees at large (PM dust, soap) — a joint manhunt / wanted page episode; where did the mosquito move to?; five SWAT officers knocked out on the man's bathroom floor; the escape report is stamped "ตามจับไม่ได้ (ลื่นเกิน)".
- EP4 (paid off in EP5: transfer granted): the mosquito has asked for a cell transfer (ยุงขอย้ายห้อง); three officers knocked out by the smell during interrogation.
- EP2: ยุง v. พัดลม — the mosquito's complaint against the fan has not been heard yet.
- EP3: PM dust at large ("นักโทษฝุ่นหลบหนีทางช่องลม"), police could set up checkpoints for dust; cell 20 is empty for a new inmate.
- The fan keeps oscillating in every episode: any new inmate in 18 or 20 is in its path.

## EP9 คดีกระจกเงา (กระจกเงาตั้งโต๊ะ)
- Charges: แอบอ้างตัวตน → ปลอมแปลงอัตลักษณ์ (two plates). Bagged + cuffed by the frame edge.
- Interrogation note on file: "ผู้ต้องหาปากดีและท้าทายเจ้าพนักงาน". Angered the judge by reflecting him: ห้องมืด 3 ปี (dark cell = nothing to reflect).
- Sentence cut for good behaviour: served as the corner mirror (กระจกส่องมุม) in the cell block, watching the guards' blind spots.
- After release: runs a makeup channel with a ring light, now lives NEXT DOOR to the man; his shower steam fogs it up. Viewers: 0.
- Hooks: the mirror holds a grudge (เงาแค้น) against the man next door; EP8's shadow + EP9's reflection = two "เงา" in one universe; the mirror saw every blind spot in the prison (could testify about the escapees).

## EP10 คดีเราเตอร์ Wi-Fi (เราเตอร์ Wi-Fi)
- Picks up from EP9's interrogation room (a router on that set is the new suspect's link to the mirror case).
- Charges: ละทิ้งหน้าที่ → ขัดขวางกระบวนการยุติธรรม (two plates; the second because the judge couldn't connect to the court Wi-Fi and lost his temper).
- The man grumbled at it; it answered with a red light (attitude). Arrested at the man's bedside table by Cyber Police; antennas tied behind its back with LAN cable. Interrogated over its blinking red light; stayed silent (ไม่ยอมส่งคลื่น).
- Sentence: a signal-proof steel box (กล่องเหล็กทึบคลื่น). Cut for good behaviour: shared its net with the guards.
- After release: jobless, its work taken by a 5G tower; all lights died.
- Hooks: a 5G tower now works in the man's neighbourhood (future suspect); the judge holds a grudge against all Wi-Fi; the guards are hooked on free net and lost it when the router left.

## EP11 คดีนาฬิกาปลุก (นาฬิกาปลุกกระดิ่งคู่)
- Way B closer: a dry consequence, no pun. Charge: ปลอมแปลงเวลาราชการ (it rang at its own 06:00 while standard time was 05:30).
- The alarm keeps ringing through the call and the raid until the bomb squad (EP2's suits) pulls its battery with giant pliers.
- National-security interrogation file: "เดินเร็วกว่าชาติ 30 นาที", statement "ติ๊ก… ต็อก…". With no battery its second hand still twitched at the lieutenant.
- Court ordered the hands removed (same pliers); cell 21, ขังลืม 5 ปี. Sentence cut: walked its laps exactly on time.
- After release: handless, ordained, runs สำนักสอนสมาธิ "อาจารย์ไร้เข็ม · ที่นี่ไม่มีเวลา". The whole country follows it, including the man, the judge, the officers and the bomb squad.
- Hooks: the man now meditates under the thing he reported (he has nothing to wake him: late for everything?); the judge and police are disciples of an ex-convict; the pulled battery and the three hands are still in evidence; cell 21 exists.
