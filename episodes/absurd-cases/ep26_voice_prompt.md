# EP26 · แก๊งห้อง 24 ตอน 7 — ผู้คุมแหกคุก — Voice prompt (Gemini TTS, voice: Fenrir)

## Style
Thai male narrator, about 35, warm deep voice, like a dramatic TV crime-documentary host who takes a ridiculous story 100% seriously. Lively and expressive, NOT flat: punch the key words, real dynamics. Never laugh: the comedy comes from how seriously he treats it. Natural spoken Thai, clear diction, steady pace, never rushed, a short breath at each comma.

Delivery per sentence:
