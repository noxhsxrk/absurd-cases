# EP22 · แก๊งห้อง 24 ตอน 3 — หมายจับคนในคุก — Voice prompt (Gemini TTS, voice: Fenrir)

## Style
Thai male narrator, about 35, warm deep voice, like a dramatic TV crime-documentary host who takes a ridiculous story 100% seriously. Lively and expressive, NOT flat: punch the key words, real dynamics. Never laugh: the comedy comes from how seriously he treats it. Natural spoken Thai, clear diction. Pace: steady, never rushed — the whole read is about 38 seconds. A short breath at each comma (about half a second) and a clear pause between sentences (about one second). Sentence 1 is calm and low, picking up where last episode left him. Sentence 2 is the reveal of who came. Sentence 3 is a rescue in progress, tense and hopeful, like a heist film. Sentence 4 is the sudden drop. Sentence 5 is a fast, helpless slide. Sentence 6 is the usual outrage. Sentences 7 and 8 are quick, then puzzled. Sentences 9 to 11 are a full police siege, big and urgent. Sentence 12 is proud, then deflated. Sentences 13 and 14 are dry. Sentence 15 is a hushed, suspenseful cliffhanger, like the last line before "to be continued".

Delivery per sentence:
1 calm and low, a tiny pause after "ชายคนนี้", then flat and a little pitiful on "ติดซี่กรงครึ่งตัว"
2 a quiet reveal on "ก้อนสบู่", pointed on "ที่เขาเคยแจ้งจับ", a breath, then almost businesslike on "มาขอเซ็นสัญญา"
3 tense and building: "มันถูตัวเขาจนลื่น", a breath, then strained and strong on "แล้วดึงสุดแรง"
4 sudden: a short stop after "แต่สบู่", then quick and falling on "ลื่นหลุดมือ"
5 fast, like he is sliding away: "เขาดีดกลับเข้าห้อง", a breath, then a flat thud on "ไปติดหน้าต่างอีกฝั่ง"
6 outraged and formal, a beat after "นั่นคือ", then read the legal term in full and with weight: "ทอดทิ้งผู้ซึ่งพึ่งตนเองมิได้!"
7 quick and decisive
8 puzzled: "สายไปติด", a breath, then surprised on "ตำรวจข้างนอก"
9 big and urgent, punch "สิบคัน", then "ล้อมคุก"
10 like a police radio report: "เจอผู้ต้องขัง", a breath, then precise on "โผล่ออกมาครึ่งตัว"
11 outraged, a beat after "นั่นคือ", then big on "หลบหนีจากที่คุมขัง!"
12 proud on "จับสบู่ได้", a breath, then deflated and dry on "แต่มันลื่นหลุดกุญแจมือ"
13 dry and official: a tiny pause after "ส่วนชายคนนี้", flat on "โดนออกหมายจับ"
14 slow and dry, a beat after "ทั้งที่", then flat on "ยังติดคุกอยู่"
15 quiet, slower, suspense creeping in: "คืนนั้น", a breath, "ห้องยี่สิบ", a beat, then low and ominous on "เริ่มส่งกลิ่น"; stop clean, leave it hanging, no trailing sound
